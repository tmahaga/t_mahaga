Why Digital ID?

Everything your campus
card does — and more.

Your TM University Digital ID is your all-in-one credential for accessing campus facilities, services, and benefits.

Instant Digital Access
Get your verified ID card in seconds. Download, save to your wallet app, or display on any device instantly after verification.
Secure & Verified
Encrypted with university-grade cryptography. Each ID carries a tamper-proof QR code validated against official student records.
Campus-Wide Acceptance
Accepted at the library, labs, sports centre, cafeteria, and all 60+ campus access points across TM University grounds.
Mobile Wallet Ready
Compatible with Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. Tap your phone to check in — no internet connection required at point of access.
Real-Time Status
Check your ID validity, renewal dates, and access privileges at any time from the student portal dashboard.
Student Discounts
Automatically unlock student pricing at partner retailers, transport providers, and software platforms with your verified ID.
